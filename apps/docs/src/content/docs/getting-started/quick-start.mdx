## Interface Overview

The application is split into dockable panels:

- **3D Viewport**: the interactive 3D view
- **Parts**: every part in the document, as a hierarchy
- **Features**: the sketch and feature history of the active part
- **Joints**: the joints connecting parts
- **Properties**: the selected part's properties

## Modeling Your First Part

1. In the **Features** panel, press **+ Sketch** and pick the plane to draw on.
2. Draw a closed shape — the sketch tools live in the viewport toolbar while
   sketch mode is active.
3. Press **Exit Sketch Mode** when the shape is done.
4. Select the sketch and press **Extrude**, then give it a distance.

The result is a solid, and every step stays in the **Features** list. Edit the
sketch later and the extrusion rebuilds from it.

## Assembling Parts

Parts can also come from outside. The **Parts** panel's toolbar offers
**Import Parts** for STL, OBJ, DAE and URDF files, **Create Primitives** for
boxes, spheres and cylinders, and **Create Empty...** for a bare node to group
things under. Once two parts exist, connect them in the **Joints** panel.

Save with **File > Save**; documents are written as `.rk` files.
